# AdventureWorks Sales Analysis | Power BI

An End-to-End Business Intelligence Project using Microsoft Power BI

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing AdventureWorks business and sales data using Microsoft Power BI to develop an interactive Business Intelligence solution.

The objective is to transform raw business data into meaningful insights through data cleaning, transformation, data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive dashboard development.

The project provides a comprehensive view of business performance across sales, products, customers, and geographical markets. It enables users to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), analyze revenue and profitability trends, evaluate product performance, understand customer behavior, and explore geographical sales distribution.

The final solution consists of multiple interactive dashboard pages designed to support business monitoring, performance evaluation, and data-driven decision-making.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze overall business and sales performance.
- Monitor key performance indicators such as Revenue, Profit, Total Orders, and Return Rate.
- Evaluate revenue and profitability across different product categories and subcategories.
- Identify top-performing products and analyze product return performance.
- Understand customer distribution, purchasing behavior, and revenue contribution.
- Analyze geographical sales distribution across countries and continents.
- Compare actual business performance against defined targets.
- Develop interactive dashboards to communicate business insights effectively.

## Project Workflow

The project was developed through the following analytical workflow:

1. **Data Preparation:** Imported the AdventureWorks datasets into Power BI and examined the available tables and fields.

2. **Data Cleaning and Transformation:** Used Power Query to prepare the data, handle data quality issues, and organize tables for analysis.

3. **Data Modeling:** Established relationships between relevant tables to create an analytical data model.

4. **DAX Development:** Created measures and calculations to evaluate revenue, profitability, orders, returns, customer performance, and business targets.

5. **Dashboard Development:** Designed interactive dashboards using appropriate charts, tables, KPI cards, gauges, slicers, and geographical visualizations.

6. **Business Analysis:** Used the dashboards to explore performance trends, product contribution, customer behavior, and geographical sales distribution.

## Dashboard Overview

The Power BI report contains four major analytical dashboards.

### 1. Executive Dashboard

Provides a consolidated overview of overall business performance through key performance indicators and interactive visualizations.

**Key Features:**
- KPI Cards displaying Total Revenue, Total Profit, Total Orders, and Return Rate.
- Revenue trend analysis across different time periods.
- Revenue comparison across product subcategories.
- Top 10 Products ranked by revenue.
- Revenue performance comparison against previous-month revenue.
- Interactive Product Category slicer.
- KPI-based business performance monitoring.

**Business Purpose:** To provide management with a quick overview of business performance, identify important revenue trends, and monitor key business metrics.

### 2. Product Dashboard

Focuses on product performance, profitability, revenue targets, order targets, and product return trends.

**Key Features:**
- Revenue Target Gauge.
- Total Orders Target Gauge.
- Return Rate Target Gauge.
- Profit and Adjusted Profit trend analysis using a line chart.
- Return Rate trend analysis using an area chart.
- Interactive Product Category slicer.
- Adjusted Price filtering.

**Business Purpose:** To evaluate product performance, monitor business targets, understand profitability trends, and analyze product return behavior.

### 3. Customer Dashboard

Provides insights into customer distribution, purchasing behavior, customer segmentation, and revenue contribution.

**Key Features:**
- Total Customers KPI.
- Revenue per Customer KPI.
- Customer income-level analysis using a pie chart.
- Customer education analysis using a donut chart.
- Customer-level performance table.
- Top customer identification based on revenue.
- Customer trend analysis over time.

**Business Purpose:** To understand customer contribution to business revenue, identify valuable customers, and evaluate customer purchasing patterns.

### 4. Map Analysis

Provides a geographical view of business revenue distribution across different countries and geographical regions.

**Key Features:**
- Geographical revenue visualization by country.
- Interactive Continent slicer.
- Revenue analysis across geographical markets.
- Dynamic geographical filtering.

**Business Purpose:** To explore geographical sales distribution, compare market contributions, and understand business performance across different regions.
